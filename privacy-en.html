What it is

The moment you save something, you forget where it is.

A screenshot is the fastest way to "save it for later" on your phone — but it has no home. Once it's in your camera roll, it looks like the other few thousand. This app turns filing into a two-minute game: 20 screenshots at a time, done when you're done, and you can stop whenever you like. Once filed, you can find any of them again by the words inside.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

We don't collect any of your data.

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Your photos and everything you organize stay on your own phone.

What we read

  • The screenshots in your photo library. Only after you grant photo access. We read them to display, sort and search them.
  • The text inside your screenshots. Recognized on your phone with Apple's built-in Vision framework to power search. Recognition happens entirely on the device — neither images nor text ever leave your phone.

Where data is stored

  • Your folders, sorting history and recognized text are stored in the app's own sandbox and UserDefaults.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and we have no server that could receive it.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data too.

Deleting photos

When you choose to delete screenshots, the app sends a delete request through Apple's PhotoKit, and iOS shows a system confirmation that you approve yourself. Deleted photos go to the "Recently Deleted" album on your iPhone, where you can recover them for 30 days. We cannot delete any photo without your confirmation.

Purchases

The app offers one one-time in-app purchase (Pro). Payment is handled by Apple; we never see any of your payment information and don't know who you are. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID — on a new phone, use "Restore Purchase" to get it back.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal data from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Because no data ever leaves the device by design, we don't expect substantive changes.

Support

The app doesn't show my screenshots

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Screenshot Tidy and make sure access is allowed. If you chose "Limited Access", only the photos you selected will appear. You can also tap "Rescan Photos" in the app's Me tab.

Search doesn't find anything

Filed screenshots are read into text in the background before they can be searched. The Me tab shows the progress; if it isn't finished, tap "Read the rest now".

I put something in the wrong folder

Open Library — tap it in "Just filed" at the top to change it. Or open a folder and tap "Select" in the top-right to move several at once.

Can I get back a screenshot I trashed?

Yes. Trashing only moves it to the app's Trash; open it from the Me tab and tap "Put back". Even after you confirm deletion, the photo stays in your iPhone's "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days.

Pro disappeared after I switched phones

Tap "Restore Purchase" in the Me tab while signed in with the same Apple ID.
